CSPC Lost & Found

About Back2You

Find what's lost, return what's found, and let it all come Back2You.

Back2You gives the CSPC community one simple place to report lost belongings, share found-item information, and help items make their way back to their owners.

Simple process

How Back2You works

Back2You brings campus listings and item reports together in one place. Start by checking the listings; if you cannot find what you need, add a clear report so others can help.

🔍

1. Search the listings

Browse lost and found items. Narrow results by item type, category, campus location, or status.

📝

2. Report an item

Choose whether you lost or found something. Add its name, category, campus location, description, and your contact details. Photos are optional and must be JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP files no larger than 5 MB.

✅

3. Verify and return it

If a found listing may match your lost-item report, sign in to submit ownership details. Keep one identifying detail private to help confirm you're the real owner - a possible match alone is not proof. You and the finder can then use a temporary conversation to coordinate returning the item.

For registered accounts

Extra ways to protect your belongings

Signing in unlocks private tools that are not part of the public lost-and-found list.

A private belongings vault

Register items you own and keep their records in your account. These belongings are not shown in the public item listings.

QR stickers for lost and found reports

Unclaimed lost and found reports can have a QR sticker that opens the item listings without exposing private contact details. Claimed reports and private registered belongings do not have a QR sticker available in the app.

Account alerts

Messages from people who scan your item's QR code appear in your account notifications, so they can reach you without seeing your phone number or email address.

Messenger alerts

Open the profile menu in Settings and connect our Back2You Facebook Page to also receive possible-match and handover alerts in Messenger, alongside email.

Student self-registration uses a CSPC student email ending in @my.cspc.edu.ph. Your password must be at least 8 characters and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special symbol.

Important to know

Before you report or claim an item

QR codes are for messages, not tracking

Scanning a code does not show an item's live location. Back2You helps people connect, but cannot guarantee that an item will be found or returned.

Share only what's needed

Use accurate contact details in reports, and avoid putting passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information in an item description.
