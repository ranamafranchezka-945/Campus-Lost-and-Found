CSPC Lost & Found

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Back2You is a lost-and-found system for the Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges (CSPC) community. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have - including when you connect Messenger to receive alerts.

Information we collect

Account information: your name, CSPC email address, student ID or department, course/year, and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.

Item reports: the title, category, description, campus location, optional photo, and contact details you choose to include when you report a lost or found item, or register a belonging.

Claim details: if you claim a found item, the identifying detail you provide to confirm ownership, and a contact number you choose to share with the other party.

Messenger information (only if you connect it): your Messenger Page-Scoped ID (PSID) and the one-time linking code you send to our Facebook Page. We never receive your Facebook password, friends list, posts, or profile beyond what Messenger shares when you message our Page.

Location (only during an approved handover): if you and the other party agree to meet up for a confirmed claim, either of you may choose to share your current location once. It is visible only to that one other person and is automatically deleted after one hour, or sooner if you stop sharing.

Technical information: standard session cookies used to keep you signed in, and basic server logs used to keep the service secure and reliable.

How we use this information

  • Matching your lost or found item reports against other reports on campus.
  • Letting you verify and claim a found item, and arranging a handover with the finder.
  • Sending you notifications about possible matches, claim updates, and handover requests - by email, and by Messenger if you have connected it.
  • Allowing campus administrators to review claims, respond to abuse reports, and keep the listings accurate.
  • Keeping the service secure (for example, rate-limiting and fraud prevention).

Messenger notifications

Facebook's Messenger Platform only allows a Page to message someone who has messaged that Page first, so connecting Messenger alerts is entirely opt-in and happens like this:

  1. From your Back2You account menu, you generate a one-time linking code.
  2. You send that code to the Back2You Facebook Page in Messenger.
  3. Our server matches the code to your account and stores your Messenger PSID so we can message you.

Once connected, we use Messenger only to deliver the same kind of account alerts we send by email: possible-match notifications and handover location requests. We do not send marketing messages, we do not post on your behalf, and we do not share your Messenger information with any third party other than Meta's own Messenger Platform, which we rely on to deliver the message.

You can disconnect Messenger at any time from your account menu. Once disconnected, we stop messaging you and delete the stored PSID.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • With the other party in a matched lost/found report, limited to what that report or claim needs (for example, a found item's reported location, or a claim's contact number).
  • With campus administrators, to review claims and moderate reports.
  • With service providers that help us run Back2You: our email provider (to deliver notification emails) and Meta's Messenger Platform (only for students who have connected Messenger alerts).

Your choices, retention, and deletion

You can edit your profile, disconnect Messenger, and delete your account at any time from your account settings. Deleting your account removes your personal information; item reports you made that are relevant to other students' claims are anonymised rather than deleted outright, so the lost-and-found history others rely on stays accurate.

A shared handover location is deleted automatically after one hour, or immediately if you choose to stop sharing it.

Security and eligibility

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, not in plain text. The service is restricted to members of the CSPC community who sign up with a CSPC email address, and administrator actions are logged for accountability.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

Questions about your data?

Contact the Back2You administrator at admin@cspc.edu.ph.
